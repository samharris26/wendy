## Apple Family Sharing and its frills

If you’re knee-deep in Apple’s ecosystem, you’re probably familiar with Family Sharing. An Apple-only wonderland, it lets you share photos, Apple Music, and even a calendar. Instant harmony, right? Well, sort of. In theory, it means one central calendar for everyone with an iPhone. You just need to grab your phone or tablet and:

- Open the Calendar app
- Tap on 'Calendars' at the bottom
- Tap the ‘Add Calendar’ button
- Give it a name and colour (anything but teal, it's everyone’s first choice)
- Tap ‘Add Person’ and choose from your contacts

Done. A shared space that, in a perfect world, solves "I didn’t know we were going to your mum's this weekend."

But what if your partner’s got a thing for Android? Or landed a work phone that’s Google-based? Congrats, welcome to cross-platform chaos.

## Google Calendar: one calendar to rule them all?

Google Calendar is the great leveller. It doesn’t matter if you're team iOS or team Android—Google's got you covered. To share with someone who's using an Android or has a Google-centric work life, try this:

1. Open Google Calendar on desktop (because the mobile app makes you jump through hoops)
2. On the left, find ‘My Calendars’, hover over the one you want to share
3. Click on the three dots > Settings and sharing
4. Scroll to ‘Share with specific people’
5. Hit ‘Add People’ and type in your family member’s email (just hope they know how to check it)

They get an email invitation, they accept, and voilà. All of a sudden, maybe, those clashing schedules will finally reveal themselves before mid-meltdown.

## The tangled web of cross-platform cohabitation

Now, if you’re in a household where one swears by Siri while the other cheers for "OK Google," you’re living in the real-life equivalent of the Matrix—with slightly fewer existential crises.

Consider setting up a unified digital view, and this is where things get messy. You can view GoogleCalendars on iOS devices by:

- Going to Settings on your iPhone
- Tapping 'Passwords & Accounts' > 'Add Account' > 'Google'
- Sign in and sync your events

## Co-parenting and childcare swaps: calendar jigsaw masters

Whether you’re co-parenting or swapping shifts with the two up the road whose kids are best pals with yours, another layer of complexity adds itself to the scheduling gymnastics. Here’s where you keep ’em sweet: Google Calendar again becomes indispensable:

- Ensure everyone involved has access to the shared calendar
- Set event reminders for pick-ups and drop-offs
- Colour-code calendar events (purple’s nice) to differentiate between different sets of kids or households
