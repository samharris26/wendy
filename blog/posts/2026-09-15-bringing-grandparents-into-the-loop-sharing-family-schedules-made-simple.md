"Have you told Mum about the dental appointment tomorrow?" It's a familiar scene. You're knee-deep in post-teatime chaos when you realise you haven't looped your mother in on the latest schedule changes. The last thing you need is another panicked WhatsApp call in the morning. Keeping grandparents and the broader support network updated on the family's comings and goings can feel like a full-time job in itself. So, how can you make sharing your family schedule with grandparents painless?

## Spreadsheets and paper notes are sabotaging your sanity

Let’s start with the bare bones—the handwritten calendar by the fridge or a shared Google Sheet. These might work if your parents are tech-savvy or have the patience to constantly check for updates. But, let's be honest, once a change is made, all hell breaks loose. Suddenly you're dealing with five versions of the "definitely final" schedule, everyone buried under a mess of scribbled notes and off-kilter printouts.

Paper and basic spreadsheets simply can't keep up with the moving parts of modern family life. And they inevitably put the onus back on you to ensure everyone has the latest copy. Not exactly the kind of village we had in mind.

## When tech doesn’t come naturally

For some grandparents, the very thought of a shared family calendar can induce a mild form of PTSD. They’re not unfriendly towards technology, they just prefer living life one day at a time. So, how do you share the rhythm of your family’s life without giving them digital anxiety?

Here’s a thought: use the tools they’re already comfortable with. If they’re regular WhatsApp users, start there. Send them daily or weekly updates directly via the app. This method doesn’t require them to learn anything new, and crucially, it means you can return that tech helpdesk shirt you’ve been wearing.

## Embracing the shared calendar club

Of course, for the brave souls ready to take the plunge into digital takes on family organisation, a shared family calendar is a solid gold solution. Once they’ve warmed to the idea, it’s a lot easier to manage than you might expect. Just think: no more "Who’s picking up Molly on Wednesdays?" debates.

Apps offering a shared family calendar view allow everyone to see the big picture without any of those notorious communication breakdowns. ## Accidental evening updates and unmissed school plays

Power in the pocket. That’s where we’re heading. Harnessing tech doesn’t mean ignoring those who find it tricky, it means making it work for everyone. Whether your aim is daily updates on WhatsApp or a colour-coded shared calendar, remember flexibility is your biggest weapon.
