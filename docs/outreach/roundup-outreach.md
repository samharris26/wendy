# Roundup outreach kit

Getting Noa into the articles that already list family calendar apps. Written
2026-09-27, after the Search Console review: 763 external links, of which 647
are Reddit (no authority passed), 90 Apple and 16 Product Hunt. Essentially no
editorial links, which is the ceiling on every non-brand search term.

Each placement is worth a link, referral traffic and App Store downloads.

## Read this first: most "best family calendar app" articles are competitors

Searching the phrase returns mostly content marketing by rival apps — Maple,
Maxie, Nuet, Calendara, Sense, Parendipity, Motion, ClickUp, Lifestack,
Fluidwave, Morgen. **Don't pitch those.** They will never add you, and asking
tells a competitor you're paying attention.

The targets below are independent: they review apps they don't sell.

## The rules

- One email per site, to a named person where the byline gives one.
- Never ask for a link. Offer the information and let them decide.
- No attachments. Link to the [press facts](#the-facts-to-paste) instead.
- One follow-up after ten days, then stop.
- Never pay for a placement: paid links breach Google's guidelines and put
  the site's rankings at risk, not just yours.
- Track each one with its own App Store campaign: `?pt=128639431&ct=pr-<site>`.

## Before you send

1. Read the article. One specific sentence referring to it beats any template.
2. Find a name and a real address (byline, About page, LinkedIn).
3. Send from your own address, not a no-reply.
4. Record what you sent and when, so the follow-up isn't a surprise.
